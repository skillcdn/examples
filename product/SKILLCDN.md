# Rules for product skills

These add to the rules of the repository.

**Evidence apart from inference.** What was observed (interviews, data, tickets, feedback) is kept apart from what was inferred, and every inference names its evidence.

**Assumptions in writing.** A specification, plan or roadmap lists the assumptions it rests on and the questions still open, so the reader can check them before building.

**The problem in the user's words.** Problem statements keep the language of the people who have the problem. Solution language comes after, and stays separate.
